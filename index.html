What we do in this course

We start at a single bit and work upward. Along the way we take apart the machine: how a photo becomes numbers, how three logic gates turn into arithmetic, how a processor executes billions of instructions a second, and how a message crosses the world in a fraction of one. No prior programming experience is expected or required.
